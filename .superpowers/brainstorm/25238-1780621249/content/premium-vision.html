🎯 Premium Design Innovations

1. Streak as Flame Orb — Not a boring number. A glowing, animated fire orb with pulsing rings. Fire = life force, energy, Japanese spirit.

2. Floating Kanji Atmosphere — 武 (martial), 魂 (spirit), 影 (shadow) drift slowly as decorative elements. Gives depth and cultural authenticity without cliché.

3. 8-Color Palette — Akane Red Vermilion Sunset Gold Violet Jade Green Teal

4. Bento Grid KPIs — Asymmetric layout, not uniform grid. Full-width rank card + 2x2 + 2x1 staggered. Each has colored accent line + gradient fill.

5. Sensei Hologram — Floating character with rotating glow rings. Purple gradient like a sci-fi hologram. Tap → opens full chat.

6. 3D Depth Everywhere — Every card has: gradient background, accent border, top glow line, shadow elevation. Hover = lift on desktop, press = scale on mobile.

7. Diegetic Tab Bar — Kanji-only icons with dot indicator. Active tab glows red. No label text clutter. Minimal and purposeful.

8. Particle System — 8 floating light orbs with different colors, sizes, and drift speeds. Creates atmosphere without performance cost.

9. Battle Cry Badge — Glowing orange pulse animation with breathing border. Creates urgency without being intrusive.

10. Dynamic Lighting — Background uses radial gradients positioned to simulate light sources from the dojo environment. Colors shift subtly.
